Title: Gender of the chief executive
Subtitle: "Chief executive" is defined here as the head of state or the head of government, whoever has more power over the appointment and dismissal of cabinet ministers.
Note: This is recorded on December 31 of each year. When the head of state and head of government hold equal power, the head of government is considered the chief executive.
