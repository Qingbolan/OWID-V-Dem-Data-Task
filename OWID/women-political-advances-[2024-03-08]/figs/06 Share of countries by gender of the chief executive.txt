Figure 06: 06 Share of countries by gender of the chief executive
Article: Women have made major advances in politics — but the world is still far from equal (2024-03-08)

Title: Share of countries by gender of the chief executive
Subtitle: "Chief executive" is defined here as the head of state or the head of government, whoever has more power over the appointment and dismissal of cabinet ministers.
Note: This is recorded on December 31 of each year. When the head of state and head of government hold equal power, the head of government is considered the chief executive.
Source: V-Dem (2026)
Units: countries
Chart: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-of-countries-by-gender-of-the-chief-executive

Text before the figure: The next chart shows how underrepresented women nonetheless are in the highest office. At any given point in time, almost all political chief executives have still been men. 2
Text after the figure: Prominent exceptions to men’s monopoly on countries’ highest offices include India’s Indira Gandhi , Bangladesh’s Sheikh Hasina , and Germany’s Angela Merkel , the heads of government of their respective countries; and Russia’s Catherine II , Liberia’s Ellen Johnson Sirleaf , and Argentina’s Cristina Fernández de Kirchner , who were their countries’ head of state.
