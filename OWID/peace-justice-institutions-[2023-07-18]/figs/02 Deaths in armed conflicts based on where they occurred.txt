Article: Promote just, peaceful and inclusive societies (2023-07-18)

Title: Deaths in armed conflicts based on where they occurred
Subtitle: Reported deaths of combatants and civilians due to fighting in [armed conflicts](#dod:armed-conflict-ucdp). Deaths due to disease and starvation resulting from the conflict are not included. Data for 2026 is incomplete and includes deaths within the first half of the year.
Note: Data for 2026 is based on quarterly reports from the source, which come with a higher level of uncertainty, and in many cases can be underestimates.
Source: Uppsala Conflict Data Program (2026); geoBoundaries (2023)
Units: deaths
Chart: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/deaths-in-armed-conflicts-by-country
