Title: Liberal political institutions
Subtitle: Data by Lührmann et al. (2018) and [V-Dem](#dod:v-dem). Expert estimates of whether the country has liberal political institutions, meaning citizens have individual and minority rights, are equal before the law, and the actions of the executive are constrained by the legislative and the courts.
