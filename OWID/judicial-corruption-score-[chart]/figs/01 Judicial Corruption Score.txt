Title: Judicial Corruption Score
Subtitle: Data by [V-Dem](#dod:v-dem). Expert estimates of how often individuals or businesses make undocumented extra payments or bribes to obtain a favorable judicial decision, or speed up or delay the process. Higher scores mean less corruption. A score of 0 is approximately the average across all countries and years.
