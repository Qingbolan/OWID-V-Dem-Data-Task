Text before the figure: In many other places, however, bribery is far more common. In some countries, more than half of those interacting with public officials report being asked for a bribe or paying one.
Text after the figure: While these surveys offer a helpful perspective — and are the official source used to track progress on the UN Sustainable Development Goals — they also have important limitations. For instance, as we explain below in more detail, people may hesitate to admit to paying a bribe and may interpret the questions differently across countries. The national surveys can also differ in their methods and coverage, which limits their comparability between countries.
