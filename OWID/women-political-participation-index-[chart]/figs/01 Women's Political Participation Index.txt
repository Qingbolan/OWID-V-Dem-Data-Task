Title: Women's Political Participation Index
Subtitle: Data by [V-Dem](#dod:v-dem). Expert estimates of the extent to which women are represented in the legislature and have an equal share of political power. The index ranges from 0 to 1 (most equal).
Note: Many countries in recent years receive high scores even though they are far from equal participation because the index scores them relative to the historical average across countries.
Source: V-Dem (2026)
