Image description: World map of the percentage of seats in the lower or single chamber of national parliaments held by women, where five countries in 2025 had more women than men and many countries have less than 25% female representation. Highlighted countries with more women than men are Rwanda (about 63.8%), Cuba (55.7%), Nicaragua (55.0%), Bolivia (50.8%), and Mexico (50.2); the United Arab Emirates is exactly 50 to 50.
