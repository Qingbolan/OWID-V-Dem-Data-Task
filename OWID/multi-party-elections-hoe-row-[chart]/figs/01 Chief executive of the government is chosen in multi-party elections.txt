Title: Chief executive of the government is chosen in multi-party elections
Subtitle: Data by Lührmann et al. (2018) and [V-Dem](#dod:v-dem). "Chief executive" is defined here as the head of state or the head of government, whoever has more power over the appointment and dismissal of cabinet ministers.
Note: When the head of state and head of government hold equal power, the head of government is considered the chief executive.
Source: V-Dem (2026)
